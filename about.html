All about me.

I like building software that connects to the physical world.

Hey y'all, I'm Cameron. I'm a computer science student at The University of Texas at Austin, interested in robotics, AI, and building systems that connect software with the physical world.

I'm pursuing a B.S. in Computer Science with a minor in Robotics, expected August 2027. My work spans robot control, embedded systems, computer vision, and software testing.

At REV Robotics, I built a multithreaded hardware-in-the-loop test system with 154 tests for the A301 motor, improved test performance, and developed Java production-testing software for motor and servo controllers. I also support students as an undergraduate course assistant for CS 314 and CS 303E.

At UT Austin, I serve in leadership with ECLAIR Robotics, helping guide robotics and AI projects from design through implementation. My other experience includes teaching at iD Tech and automation and migration engineering at Behrens and Associates.

Outside of engineering, I enjoy piano, chess, D&D, Zelda, singing, and country and West Coast swing dancing. I also replaced my keyboard keys with clear, unmarked keys; touch-typing numbers took some getting used to.
