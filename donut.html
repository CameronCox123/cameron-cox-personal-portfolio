Combat robotics · 3-lb class · Fusion 360 · EasyEDA · Bambu Studio

The robot

D.O.N.U.T. is a 3-lb translational-drift “meltbybrain” combat robot developed with the Experimental Division for Combat Robotics (EXD).

Design and build

The design and fabrication workflow used Fusion 360 for CAD, EasyEDA for electronics, and Bambu Studio for 3D printing.
